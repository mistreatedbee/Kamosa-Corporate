``` ### The important part Create this file in your project's **`public`** folder: ```text public/ ├── logo.jpg └── social-preview.jpg ``` The `social-preview.jpg` should be **1200 × 630 px**. I'd make the image look something like: **KAMOSA** *Professional Business Solutions* **Health & Safety • Compliance • Consulting • Procurement • Training** **South Africa** with the Kamosa logo and your existing brand colours. That will give WhatsApp something much more attractive to display instead of just pulling the small square `logo.jpg`. Also, once `kamosa.co.za` is actually live, change **both** the `og:url` and image URLs from `kamosa-corporate.vercel.app` to the real domain. One more important thing: **WhatsApp caches link previews.** So after deploying the change, an old preview may continue appearing for a while. Testing with a newly generated URL/query parameter can help confirm the new metadata is being read.
