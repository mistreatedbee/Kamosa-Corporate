# Kamosa Admin — Operating Guide

One page. Everything you need to run the lead inbox day to day.

---

## Logging in

1. Go to **kamosa-corporate.vercel.app/admin/login**
2. Enter the admin key (given to you separately — treat it like a password; don't share it over
   email or WhatsApp, and don't paste it anywhere public)
3. You'll land on the Enquiries list

Your session stays logged in for 8 hours, then asks you to sign in again. This is normal.

## Check daily, for now

**Until the domain is bought and email notifications are switched on, this page is the only way
you find out someone contacted you.** There is no ping to your phone or inbox yet — a new enquiry
just sits here silently. Make it a habit: check `/admin` once a day, ideally in the morning.

Once email is live, the inbox becomes your backup record rather than your only way of finding out.

## What you're looking at

The table shows every submission from the website — the contact form, quote requests, and service
requests — newest first. Use the two dropdowns at the top to filter by type or status.

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| Received | When they submitted |
| Name / Company / Contact | Who to get back to, and how |
| Type | `general` = contact form; `quote_hs` / `quote_training` / `quote_procurement` = a quote request for that service; `service_request` = "I already know what I want" |
| Service | Which Kamosa service line this relates to |
| Status | Where this lead is in your process (see below) |
| Message | What they actually wrote |
| Notes | Click to add or read your own private notes on this lead — never visible to the visitor |

## Statuses — what to set, and when

- **New** — default. Nothing done yet.
- **Contacted** — you've reached out (phone, email, WhatsApp).
- **Qualified** — it's a real, viable enquiry worth pursuing.
- **In Progress** — you're actively working it (preparing a quote, in discussion).
- **Converted** — became a client / the work happened.
- **Closed** — didn't go anywhere, but it was a real enquiry.
- **Spam** — not a real enquiry. (The site already filters obvious bots before they reach this
  list — anything you see here got past that, so use this status sparingly.)

Change it by clicking the status dropdown on that row — it saves instantly, no save button.

## Notes

Click **Notes** on any row to see or add internal notes — reminders to yourself, what you discussed
on a call, next steps. These are never shown to the person who submitted the enquiry.

## What NOT to do

- Don't share the admin key with anyone who doesn't need it. If you think it's been seen by someone
  it shouldn't have, ask for it to be rotated.
- Don't rely on this page alone for anything time-sensitive until email notifications are on —
  check it daily in the meantime.
- Don't expect editing, deleting, or exporting enquiries yet — this is intentionally a lightweight
  inbox (view + status + notes), not a full CRM. If you find yourself needing more than that
  regularly, that's the signal to ask for it — not before.
