  management plan or a safety documentation pack.

## Part 5 — Style / Voice Guide

Extracted directly from the profile's own language patterns:

- **Tone:** Professional, practical, direct. The profile favours plain statements of what is done
  ("Help organisations build, implement, and maintain...") over marketing flourish.
- **Preferred descriptive words (used by the profile itself, safe to reuse):** practical, reliable,
  professional, trusted, ethical, responsive, integrated, tailored, accredited-standard,
  hands-on, measurable value.
- **Avoid invented superlatives** not present in the profile: "industry-leading," "world-class,"
  "best-in-class," "cutting-edge," "unmatched," "#1," "award-winning" — none of these appear in the
  profile and none should be introduced.
- **Numbers:** Use only confirmed figures — "Level 1 B-BBEE," "135% procurement recognition," "100%
  Black Female Owned," "Over six years of hands-on SHE experience," "four" core/integrated service
  lines. Do not invent client counts, project counts, or percentages not in the profile.
- **Sentence structure:** The profile favours short overview sentences followed by a concrete bullet
  list — mirror this in service and industry pages rather than long unbroken paragraphs.
- **First person plural ("we") is used throughout** the MD message and About sections — keep this
  voice for company-facing copy; MD Profile section uses third person ("she," "her") — keep that
  distinction when writing about Lovedonia Mmola specifically versus the company generally.
- **Certainty language:** The profile states things plainly and does not hedge on confirmed facts
  (registration numbers, credentials) but is explicit where something is not confirmed. New content
  should follow the same pattern — state confirmed facts plainly, mark unconfirmed items explicitly
  rather than writing around them vaguely.
- **Do not use testimonials, quotes from clients, or numeric outcome claims ("reduced incidents by
  X%")** — none exist in the profile and the master checklist explicitly forbids inventing them.
