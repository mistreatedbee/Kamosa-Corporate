## Our Services

Four integrated service lines, each grounded in hands-on industry experience.

### 1. Health, Safety & Compliance (core discipline)
Help organisations build, implement, and maintain occupational health and safety systems that
satisfy the OHS Act, the Construction Regulations, and Mine Health and Safety requirements.
- Construction health and safety file development and management
- Fall Protection Plan development (certified Fall Protection Plan Developer)
- Hazard Identification and Risk Assessments (HIRA) and baseline risk assessments
- Legal compliance audits and legal appointment registers
- Incident investigation and reporting
- Emergency preparedness and response planning
- SHE management plans and safety documentation packs for tenders

### 2. Business Consulting
Practical advisory support that helps organisations strengthen systems, manage risk, and improve
operational performance.
- Management systems development and documentation (ISO-aligned)
- Policy and procedure development
- Operational risk management and internal auditing
- Contractor management and compliance support

### 3. Procurement & Supply
Reliable supply of safety-related goods to keep workplaces compliant and operational.
- Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) supply
- Construction tools and consumables supply
- Safety signage and related site materials

### 4. Training & Skills Development
Accredited-standard training delivered by a registered assessor, moderator, and facilitator,
including materials designed for workforces with varying literacy levels.
- Occupational health and safety awareness training
- HAZCHEM, safe stacking & storage, and hazardous chemical agents training
- First aid and legal liability training
- Development of learner guides, assessments, and marking memoranda

### Environmental Management (Associate Capability — not in-house)
Delivered through an associate network, drawing on the experience of a practising Environmental
Assessment Practitioner with a background at the South African National Biodiversity Institute
(SANBI).
- Environmental compliance support under NEMA, NEMBA, and related legislation
- Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) support and inputs
- Invasive alien species management and eradication planning
- Biodiversity conservation, ecosystem restoration, and natural resource management
- Stakeholder engagement and environmental awareness within communities

## Service Delivery Methodology (six stages)

1. **DISCOVER** — Engage with the client to understand business objectives, operational
   challenges, compliance obligations, and project requirements.
2. **ASSESS** — Evaluate the current environment by reviewing systems, processes, documentation,
   risks, and operational practices to identify improvement opportunities.
3. **DESIGN** — Develop practical, customised solutions aligned with the client's objectives,
   applicable legislation, and industry best practices.
4. **IMPLEMENT** — Support implementation through professional consulting, documentation,
   guidance, monitoring, and stakeholder engagement.
5. **MONITOR** — Evaluate progress, measure outcomes, and monitor effectiveness of implemented
   solutions.
6. **IMPROVE** — Work with clients to identify opportunities for ongoing enhancement, innovation,
   and operational excellence.

Guiding principles for every project: Professionalism, Quality, Integrity, Client Satisfaction.
